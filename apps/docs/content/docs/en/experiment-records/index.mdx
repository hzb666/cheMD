These chapters are written for experiment users. It follows the natural structure of a lab record: evidence, relationships, and best-practice examples.

## Record Construction Order

1. Declare molecule and reaction objects.
2. Add results and analyses.
3. Add procedures and observations with explicit step and event IDs.
4. Add samples, artifacts, and evidence chains.
5. Add condition screens, route links, and multi-document organization when the experiment requires them.
